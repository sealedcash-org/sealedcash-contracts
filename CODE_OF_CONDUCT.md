# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in SealedCash welcoming, respectful,
and harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good intent, respect differing viewpoints, and keep
technical discussions focused on the work. Do not publish private information,
credentials, or details of an unpatched security issue.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate
exposure of secrets are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to support@sealedcash.com. Reports are
reviewed by the project maintainers and handled confidentially where possible.
